Privacy policy

This site is a tacplus fleet operated by Jody Scott. Signing in uses your Google, Apple, or Microsoft account; this page says what that sign-in collects and what is done with it.

What is collected

When you sign in, your identity provider sends this site the identity it holds for you: a stable subject identifier, your email address, your name, and your profile picture URL (the OpenID Connect openid, email and profile scopes). Nothing else is requested from your account, and this site never sees your password.

What it is used for

Your identity is used to recognise you on return visits and to issue a session on this site. Signing in grants no access by itself: you can use a tenant's services only when that tenant's administrator has added you to it. Without a tenant, your sign-in records only that you signed in.

What is kept

This site keeps the identity provider's subject identifier, your email address and name, and the sessions issued to you. It uses cookies and browser storage only to hold your session. It does not sell, share, or use your identity for advertising, and it does not pass it to third parties except the identity provider you signed in with.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request to have your identity removed: jody@seabeam.net.
